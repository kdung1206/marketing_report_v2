Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Livotec & Karofi Marketing Reporting Console ("the App"), an internal marketing analytics dashboard used by Livotec and Karofi staff to report on their own brands' marketing performance. The App is not a public consumer-facing product and is not distributed to the general public.

What data we access

The App integrates with the TikTok Login Kit so that an authorized staff member can connect Livotec's or Karofi's own official TikTok account to the dashboard. Once connected, the App reads only:

  • Basic profile info of the connected account (name, username, avatar)
  • Account statistics of the connected account (follower count, following count, likes count, video count)
  • Public video list of the connected account, with per-video view/like/comment/share counts

We only ever access data belonging to the TikTok account that its owner explicitly connects through the OAuth consent screen — never any other TikTok user's data.

How we use this data

Data is used exclusively to generate internal marketing performance reports (follower growth, content engagement) for Livotec/Karofi's own marketing team. We do not use this data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to internal reporting, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

Data storage and retention

Data is stored in our private database (Supabase), accessible only to authenticated staff accounts with the appropriate role. Access tokens are stored encrypted. Data is retained for as long as the account remains connected, and is deleted when an administrator disconnects the account from the App's Control Panel, or upon request.

Revoking access

The account owner can disconnect the TikTok account at any time from the App's Control Panel, or by revoking the App's access directly from their TikTok account settings. Once disconnected, the App stops syncing new data immediately.
