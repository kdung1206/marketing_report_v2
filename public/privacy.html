Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

MetricAdPro is an internal marketing reporting console used by authorized Livotec and Karofi staff to consolidate marketing performance data from connected advertising and social platforms. The service is not a public consumer product.

Information We Access

When an authorized administrator connects a platform account, MetricAdPro may access the following data:

  • Google Ads campaign performance, including campaign names, ad group names, ad names, spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, video views, and report dates.
  • TikTok Ads reporting data, including campaign, ad group, ad, spend, impressions, clicks, reach, frequency, conversions, video views, and report dates.
  • Organic social reporting data from connected company-owned TikTok, YouTube, or Facebook accounts, such as account statistics and public content performance metrics.
  • Basic account identifiers needed to display and sync the connected accounts, such as customer IDs, advertiser IDs, channel IDs, account names, and brand labels.

Google API Data Use

MetricAdPro uses Google Ads API access only to read advertising performance data from Google Ads accounts that the user has authorized. The data is used exclusively to generate internal marketing performance reports for Livotec and Karofi. We do not sell Google user data, use it for unrelated advertising or profiling, or transfer it to third parties except as necessary to operate the internal reporting system.

How We Use Data

Data is used to build internal dashboards, compare performance by brand and channel, monitor spend and conversions, and support marketing reporting workflows. Access is limited to authenticated staff accounts with appropriate roles.

Storage and Security

Reporting data is stored in a private Supabase database. OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted before storage. Production secrets are stored in Vercel environment variables. Only authorized administrators can connect, disconnect, or sync platform accounts.

Retention and Deletion

Data is retained while the account remains connected and while it is needed for internal reporting. An administrator can disconnect an account from the Control Panel, after which MetricAdPro stops syncing new data. Deletion requests can be sent to the contact below.

Revoking Access

Users can revoke Google access at any time from their Google Account permissions page. Administrators can also disconnect connected accounts in MetricAdPro. Once access is revoked or disconnected, MetricAdPro can no longer sync new data from that account.
