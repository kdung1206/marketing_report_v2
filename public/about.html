MetricAdPro

Internal marketing reporting console for Livotec & Karofi

MetricAdPro consolidates marketing performance data from the advertising, social, and website analytics platforms Livotec and Karofi use — Facebook Ads & Page Insights, Google Ads, TikTok Ads & organic insights, YouTube channel analytics, and website analytics via Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console — into a single internal reporting dashboard.

What it does

  • Reads read-only performance metrics (spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, sessions, users, follower counts, etc.) from platform accounts an authorized administrator explicitly connects.
  • Builds weekly/monthly internal reports comparing performance by brand (Livotec, Karofi) and channel.
  • Never posts, edits, or deletes anything on a connected account — every integration uses read-only API scopes.

Who it's for

This is an internal tool for Livotec and Karofi's own marketing team, not a public consumer product or a service offered to other organizations. Access requires a staff account issued by an administrator.
